Flat solid fills only: no gradients, no textures, no realistic lighting, no 3D, no photo. Bright, clean pastel colors on a soft, simple background. Rounded, chubby shapes. Gentle, slow, bouncy motion like a kids' picture book come to life. Vertical 9:16, 720p, 24 fps, 10 seconds, one continuous shot.

No arms, no fingers, no gloves: the hands are two small plain white circles with navy outlines, like little balls, floating just beside the body at mid-height and never connected to it; to wave or hold something, a circle simply floats there.
